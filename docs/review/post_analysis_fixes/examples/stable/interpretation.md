and effective criterion `elbo`, `tol=0.001`, and
`stop_reason="criterion_satisfied"`. The final logged ELBO is
−1363.8547312974285; its last increase is 2.8121007744630333e−7. Maximum last
alpha and PIP changes are both 9.470669224898032e−5. These support numerical
stability of this trajectory, although alpha/PIP thresholds were not the selected
stopping condition. No objective decrease, nonfinite fallback, cycle or iteration
limit exit is recorded. Residual variance stays at 1; final nonzero component
variances are 0.08431651034695109 and 0.06199047237827562, with last changes
9.910537576943157e−6 and 4.892951868951112e−7. See
[compact fit fields](analysis_summary.json), `fit.last`, and
[plot records](plot_data.json), `convergence before and after.stages`.

Refinement completed one round: **2 candidate attempts, 4 inner fits, 0 accepted**.
Excluding `syn0100` gave A/B iteration counts 3/2 and a B improvement of
−6.184563972055912e−11; excluding `syn0350` gave 3/3 and
6.821210263296962e−13. Neither exceeds the absolute acceptance tolerance 0.001.
The returned PIPs and CS are unchanged. The comparison objective is the uniform
original-model objective recomputed from the complete **post-trim state**,
−1363.854753649087 before and after. It must not be substituted with logged
sweep ELBO. These attempts support local stability under this search, not global
optimality. See [summary](posterior_summary.json), `refinement.history[0:2]`,

## LD diagnosis and its limits

All 500 variants were assessed: zero flagged, zero conditional-only, zero
unassessed. SLSQP converged with KKT maximum violation 3.0325963962241076e−10;
regularization is 4.2300630066830894e−13, rank 500 and minimum eigenvalue
0.012157615498048023. The largest absolute conditional residual is `syn0350`
(−4.38563687696406), but its flip log LR is −41.00366396647055. A large residual
alone does not satisfy the strict **log LR>2 AND |adjusted z|>2** flag rule.
See [LD records](ld_diagnostics.json), `variants[variant_id=syn0350]`, `mixture`,
`thresholds`, `rank` and `minimum_eigenvalue`.

The retained [small-fixture R comparisons](../../../post_analysis/numerical/ld_oracles.json)
and [certified reference](../../../post_analysis/numerical/kriging_certified_oracle.json)
show why raw mixsqp output and an optimality-checked solution differ. They do not
bound this 500-variant example. Paired original-R and certified-reference
per-variant log LR/flags, mixture weights/KKT checks, and identical adjusted z,
LD identity, regularization and thresholds are missing for this exact example.
Consequently these records cannot determine whether the R discrepancy would
change its flags. No larger validation was started to fill this gap.
