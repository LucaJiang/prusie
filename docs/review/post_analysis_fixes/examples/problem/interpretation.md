# Problem example: a stable inner fit can have a better refinement candidate

Generated by the current Codex session for human scientific review. This is a
direct reading of saved numerical records and PNGs, separate from the optional
AI adapter. [Provenance](provenance.json) distinguishes the original fits and LD
diagnostics from the bounded L=12 refit and new rendering. Variant labels in this
official convergence fixture do not support biological mechanism claims.

which was not its selected stopping rule. Residual variance's last changes are
−8.656383460037276e−7 and −3.135608899196285e−7. The initial fit has ten active

199.4–199.9 out of 200. After refinement only components 1 and 2 remain active,

Refinement made **5 attempts / 10 inner fits / 2 rounds**, accepting one candidate.
The common objective is the uniform original-model objective recomputed from the
complete **post-trim state**: −70832.27160472395 → −70814.81329542199, an improvement
of **17.45830930196098**. The logged-ELBO difference instead is
17.458333143775235; the phase/trim distinction must remain explicit.

See [comparison](comparison.json), `variants` keyed by `variant_id` and `pips`.
The initial singleton CS `{35}` and `{32}` become `{33}`; the `{86,90}` CS remains.
This is a substantial change in the supported variant sets, not just redistribution
inside one unchanged CS. The formal comparison records one matched pair and three
unmatched endpoints for baseline versus refinement. The retained pair's component
overlap is 0.9925405245291377. Moderate correlations of 33 with 32/35
(r=0.5996648929848706 / 0.5642377546607126) alone do not establish that their
conditional effects are indistinguishable.

Refined component 1's pair has posterior mass 0.9999999912437146,
effective candidates 1.5953381386626992, and |r|=0.9989091776612095. Thus the
0.823-versus-0.177 probability split still leaves LD-limited localization.
Component 2 concentrates on 33. Correlation between 33 and 90 is
0.5321733801522945; this is genotype correlation, not proof that the two modeled
effects cannot be distinguished conditionally. No outside-CS proxy meets |r|≥0.99;
79 is correlated with 86/90 at about 0.90 but has refined PIP
8.756285363453742e−9. Sources: [CS table](tables/credible_sets.csv),
[posterior summary](posterior_summary.json), `proxies`, and
[historical selected LD](../../../post_analysis/examples/problem/selected_ld.json).
CS count is not a known causal count.

L=12 preserves all three baseline CS and gives three formal matched relations;
its nine unretained active components remain in the complete component layer.
The full-region maximum PIP change is 0.00969418571478775 at variant `0`
(0.038560306751959095 → 0.048254492466746846), consistent with a change in the
diffuse background rather than a new retained CS. All **200 variants are present
in all settings**: refined zeros for 32/35 are low modeled probabilities, not
omitted variants. The complete component evidence remains available without
turning uniform posterior overlap into identified split/merge events.

## LD diagnosis and uncertainty

All 200 variants were assessed, with **zero flagged, conditional-only or
unassessed**. The mixture's KKT maximum violation is 1.5091927707544528e−10.
Regularization is 4.2300630066830894e−13; rank is 200 at r_tol=1e−8, with minimum
eigenvalue 3.477986166282123e−8. This near-singularity warrants checking input LD
and z alignment, rather than an automatic allele flip. Variant 33 has the largest
absolute conditional residual (−3.871962268434617) but log LR −90.95196456810982.
Variant `0` has log LR 3.5595496960231547 but |adjusted z|≈0.644, failing the
second part of **log LR>2 AND |adjusted z|>2**. See
[LD records](ld_diagnostics.json), `variants` by ID, `mixture`, `thresholds`,
`rank`, `minimum_eigenvalue` and `r_tol`.

The [raw R](../../../post_analysis/numerical/ld_oracles.json) and
[certified-reference](../../../post_analysis/numerical/kriging_certified_oracle.json)
checks concern small fixtures, not this exact 200-variant matrix. Paired original-R
and certified per-variant log LR/flags, mixture weights and optimality checks,
with matched adjusted z, LD identity, sample size, regularization and thresholds,
are absent here. The saved evidence cannot establish whether those numerical
differences would change this example's flags. No new large validation was run.
