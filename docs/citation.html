Citation and license

Cite Wang et al. (2020) for the SuSiE model and fitting method. For analyses using association summary statistics and linkage disequilibrium (LD), also cite Zou et al. (2022) for SuSiE-RSS. The statistical model page describes these methods.

  • Wang G, Sarkar A, Carbonetto P, Stephens M (2020). A simple new approach to variable selection in regression, with application to genetic fine mapping. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Statistical Methodology), 82(5):1273–1300. DOI: 10.1111/rssb.12388.
  • Zou Y, Carbonetto P, Wang G, Stephens M (2022). Fine-mapping from summary data with the “Sum of Single Effects” model. PLOS Genetics, 18(7):e1010299. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pgen.1010299.
