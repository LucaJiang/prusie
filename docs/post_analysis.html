Convergence and refinement

A stopping rule tells you whether one IBSS trajectory has settled under its selected criterion. Continuation gives an unfinished trajectory more iterations; refinement searches for a better solution from different initial states. Read these alongside PIPs and credible sets, then compare justified alternative settings.

Start with a tracked fit

import prusie
from prusie.plots import plot_convergence, plot_refinement

example = prusie.load_example()
fit = prusie.susie_rss(**example["inputs"], **example["parameters"],
                       track_fit="compact", retain_data=True)
print(fit.convergence["stop_reason"])
figure, axes, plotted = plot_convergence(fit, layout="compact")

For this toy input the selected ELBO rule is satisfied after three sweeps. The worked examples also show a ten-sweep fit that satisfies its rule yet improves substantially under refinement.

The compact figure shows ELBO, signed ΔELBO, Δalpha and ΔPIP. Use layout="variance" for V and residual variance, or layout="full" for all six metrics. Selected stopping thresholds and diagnostic-only thresholds have different line styles. A missing trace stays unavailable; it is not reconstructed from the final fit.

Tracking is opt-in and does not select a different stopping rule. retain_data=True owns a read-only copy of the validated matrix and scale metadata for explicit continuation and refinement; tracking or initialization also retains those inputs. Ordinary calls with these options disabled do not retain the matrix.

Read the stopping evidence

For biological variant jjj, marginal PIP combines only active components:

PIPj=1−∏l:Vl>prior_tol(1−αlj). \mathrm{PIP}_j = 1-\prod_{l:V_l>\mathrm{prior\_tol}}(1-\alpha_{lj}). PIPj​=1−l:Vl​>prior_tol∏​(1−αlj​).

The internal null column is excluded. No active components gives zero PIP. Component alpha and marginal PIP are different: components can exchange their probability allocations while PIP stays unchanged.

At the end of each complete sequential IBSS sweep, before any residual variance update, prusie records ELBO and exact distances from the preceding sweep:

Δα=max⁡l,j∣αlj(t)−αlj(t−1)∣,ΔPIP=max⁡j∣PIPj(t)−PIPj(t−1)∣. \Delta_\alpha=\max_{l,j}|\alpha^{(t)}_{lj}-\alpha^{(t-1)}_{lj}|,\qquad \Delta_{\mathrm{PIP}}=\max_j|\mathrm{PIP}^{(t)}_j-\mathrm{PIP}^{(t-1)}_j|. Δα​=l,jmax​∣αlj(t)​−αlj(t−1)​∣,ΔPIP​=jmax​∣PIPj(t)​−PIPj(t−1)​∣.

Each PIP uses its own iteration's activity state. Alpha distance includes null; PIP distance does not. The posterior-mean distance uses bˉj=∑lαljμlj\bar b_j=\sum_l\alpha_{lj}\mu_{lj}bˉj​=∑l​αlj​μlj​ on the working predictor scale. Signed variance changes, top variants, entropy, activity and events accompany these metrics. Online entropy includes the null column; the posterior resolution table instead normalizes biological alpha and reports null mass separately.

The first sweep has no preceding recorded sweep: its distances are unavailable. fit.convergence includes requested/effective criterion, thresholds, iteration count, stop reason, last distances, nonfinite/decrease events and finalization. fit.converged and fit.ibss_converged describe the returned inner IBSS fit.

The default finite-ELBO rule, starting at iteration two, is 0≤ΔELBO<tol0\leq\Delta\mathrm{ELBO}<\mathrm{tol}0≤ΔELBO<tol. Even a small negative increment does not satisfy it. Relative change is separately reported as ΔELBO/max⁡(1,∣ELBOt−1∣)\Delta\mathrm{ELBO}/\max(1,|\mathrm{ELBO}_{t-1}|)ΔELBO/max(1,∣ELBOt−1​∣) and is not a default stopping condition. A nonfinite increment uses a distinctly labelled alpha/PIP fallback; a nonfinite objective is never eligible for refinement selection.

rule = prusie.ConvergenceConfig(criterion='joint', alpha_tol=1e-6,
                                 pip_tol=1e-6, min_iter=2, cycle_lag=5)
fit = prusie.susie_rss(**example['inputs'], **example['parameters'],
                       convergence=rule, track_fit=True)

alpha_pip requires both probability distances to be strictly below their separate tolerances. joint also requires the finite-ELBO condition. Tolerances for alpha and PIP are absolute probability units; ELBO tolerance is an objective increment. A lag-one fixed point is distinct from a short cycle. A detected cycle of lag two through five retains the complete current posterior and reports cycle_detected, with IBSS convergence false. Alpha is never averaged while leaving its moments or variance parameters from another state.

After a non-stopping sweep, residual variance may be updated. Final trimming then zeros components with Vl<prior_tolV_l<\mathrm{prior\_tol}Vl​<prior_tol, including at the iteration limit. Historical elbo and XtXr fields remain at their established phases; evaluate_objective(fit) explicitly recomputes likelihood, KL and fitted products from the complete saved state. convergence['finalization'] separates trim changes from IBSS iterations. The CS activity threshold remains 10−910^{-9}10−9; a custom PIP prior_tol can therefore produce different boundary activity.

Compact tracking (True or 'compact') records exact online distances and component summaries. Its alpha display retains rows with maximum alpha at least 2/p2/p2/p and entries at least 1/p1/p1/p, where ppp includes any internal null column. Thresholds, omitted mass and lossy=True are explicit. This display cannot reconstruct the posterior. 'dense' also records full alpha and moments and is intended for small debugging examples. No history is fabricated when tracking was disabled.

Continue first, then consider refinement

An iteration-limit fit is unfinished under its stopping rule. Explicit continuation can finish its inner optimization:

continued = prusie.continue_fit(fit, max_iter=100, tol=1e-5, track_fit=True)

Continuation inherits the original requested convergence configuration unless you supply convergence=ConvergenceConfig(...). An omitted continuation tol also inherits the fit's ELBO tolerance; an explicit value overrides it. Refinement inherits the same alpha/PIP thresholds, minimum iteration count and cycle controls; inner_tol sets its separate inner ELBO tolerance. Runtime fallback from a nonfinite ELBO is recorded as an effective rule and is not reused as the next requested rule. Older saved fits without the requested configuration use a labelled compatibility resolution; the result records resolved controls rather than claiming exact inheritance. Inspect per-stage solver_settings and convergence for each refinement A/B fit.

Alternatively, pass s_init=fit to the same-data fitting interface. Variant IDs/order, supplied allele labels, null layout, working scales and the data signature must agree. Unknown cross-input transformations are rejected. A minimal state mapping must explicitly supply alpha, mu, mu2, variant_ids, scales, null_index and data_signature, plus allele metadata when present. Optional V and sigma2 are checked; estimated parameters can resume their values, while fixed parameters follow the requested model. Growing L retains the first components and adds zero-mean prior rows; shrinking L keeps the first L. Fitted crossproducts are rebuilt from the current posterior and inputs. Old KL, BF and fitted caches are never trusted.

Refinement searches other solutions after a valid converged baseline:

refined = prusie.refine_fit(fit, prusie.RefinementConfig(
    max_rounds=10, absolute_tol=1e-3, relative_tol=None,
    max_iter=100, inner_tol=1e-4, candidate_budget=20))

For every retained CS in the round's fixed incumbent:

  1. Temporarily zero its variant weights, normalize the remaining full prior including null, and fit from a cold start. Zero support is a hard exclusion.
  2. Restore the exact original prior and model, transfer only alpha/mu/mu2, and refit. V and sigma2 restart at the original model initialization.

The best finite, valid, converged stage-B candidate is accepted only if its common recomputed original-prior post-trim objective improves by more than the absolute tolerance. Stage A has a different prior and cannot win this comparison. Candidate states share immutable input matrices but own their mutable posterior arrays. A failed final attempt never replaces an earlier accepted best.

The default absolute threshold avoids accepting numerical noise. The optional relative threshold is disabled because its scale depends on sample size and objective constants. A candidate budget counts exclusion attempts; an attempt uses at most two fits. Round or candidate limits report budget_exhausted, not a completed search. refinement_status, refinement_stop_reason, per-stage stops/times, original CS identities, candidate improvements and before/after summaries are separate from IBSS convergence. No CS, no improvement, unsupported baseline, all candidates failed, and exhausted budgets have distinct outcomes. No improvement describes this search only, not all possible local optima.

evaluate_objective follows the fitting convention log(pi + sqrt(eps)) for ordinary priors and zero KL for trimmed V=0 components. This explains small logged-versus-final differences. Refinement deliberately corrects upstream's approximate CS exclusions: the temporary stage-A zero weights remain exactly zero. Returned default standard fits retain the established epsilon convention.

Compare before and after

refined = prusie.refine_fit(fit)
convergence_figure = plot_convergence(fit, after=refined, layout="compact")
search_figure = plot_refinement(fit, refined, layout="compact")

Before and returned-after trajectories use separate iteration axes. If the search accepted no improvement, the label is After search (unchanged). The refinement view shows candidate PIPs, stage-B improvements relative to each round's incumbent, and the accepted objective path. Full attempt logs distinguish failures, unconverged candidates, gains below the acceptance threshold, valid candidates that did not win, and accepted candidates.

Use the recomputed post-trim original-model objective to assess the gain. The final logged sweep ELBO has a different phase and can differ slightly. Candidate attempts are separate solutions; connecting their PIPs across variant positions would not describe a fitted curve. layout="full" and the report's complete tables expose additional variants and component relations.

Compare explicit settings

Continue with sensitivity and LD diagnostics for L, prior variance, variant priors, windows and report-only CS thresholds. Requested convergence controls are inherited by sensitivity fits; an explicit override is recorded. Runtime fallback never silently becomes the next requested stopping rule.

Figures, persistence and optional interpretation

See the quick start for HTML, figure and lossless data exports, and the plotting API for layouts and local styles. Reports summarize deterministic results without fitting again. Optional AI interpretation remains separate from those results and requires an explicit request and consent; offline analysis needs no model service.
